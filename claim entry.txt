### Important observation

There are quite a few fields where **`name` exists but `id` does not**, especially in `editForm` and both `detailForm`s. There are also **duplicate IDs**, particularly `queryImgClKy`, `queryImgClapKy`, `queryImgClbcKy`, `queryImgClvtKy`, `queryImgGidcKy`, `formuser`, and `queryImgform`. This matters if the JavaScript accesses fields using `$('#id')`, because it can target the wrong element.
